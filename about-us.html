Shree Balaji Infra is Maharashtra's premier manufacturer and converting specialist of high-tensile PPGI & Galvalume roofing sheets, structural C & Z purlins, and customized pre-engineered building envelopes engineered for extreme monsoon weather resistance.

WHO WE ARE

Engineered For Strength.
Crafted For Generations.

Established in 2016, Shree Balaji Infra has developed into one of western India's most trusted manufacturers, fabricators, and converting specialists for industrial roofing sheets, secondary structural purlins, and pre-engineered building (PEB) solutions. Headquartered in Uruli Devachi, Pune, our modern production facility serves heavy industrial corridors across Chakan, Ranjangaon, Talegaon, Baramati, Mumbai, and pan-Maharashtra.

Under the visionary technical guidance of our founder, Mr. Vijay Madhukar Shinde, we have built our reputation on an uncompromising foundation of prime mill sourcing (Tata Steel, JSW, Jindal, SAIL), continuous computerized roll profiling, and surgical cut-to-length customization up to 14 meters that virtually eliminates on-site cutting scrap.

“Our core engineering philosophy relies on precision from coil intake to jobsite dispatch. When builders choose Shree Balaji Infra, they invest in verified mill metallurgy, watertight profile geometry, and decades of leak-free service.”

Vijay Madhukar Shinde — Managing Director & Chief Engineer

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Engineered Precision Across Our Expertise

Equipped with multi-stand roll forming profiling lines, CNC crimping systems, and structural purlin pre-punching, we engineer specialized steel products built for heavy industrial spans.

HIGH-TENSILE 550 MPA

PPGI & Galvalume Roofing Sheets

Continuous roll formed trapezoidal and hi-rib profile sheets engineered with capillary anti-siphon side grooves, ensuring 100% watertight roof assemblies even under intense torrential monsoon rainfall.

Thickness: 0.30mm – 0.80mm
Tensile: 550 MPa (Certified)
Length: Cut-to-length up to 14 meters
CNC CRIMPED RADIUS

Architectural Crimp Curved Sheets

Precision convex and concave crimped curved sheets manufactured on CNC equipment for seamless warehouse canopies, factory barrel vaults, and aircraft hangars without central ridge joints.

Min Radius: 300 mm
Profiles: Hi-Rib & Regular Rib
Joints: Completely seamless ridgeless
GRADE E350 STEEL

Structural C & Z Purlins

Cold-formed high-strength secondary framing purlins with computerized pre-punched bolt holes for rapid, weld-free on-site bolted assembly with optimal weight-to-load structural ratios.

Depth: 100mm – 300mm
Thickness: 1.5mm – 3.0mm
Punching: Automated CNC pre-punched
THERMAL INSULATION

Sandwich PUF Panels & Flashings

High-density rigid polyurethane foam insulated panels for cold-storage facilities, controlled atmosphere sheds, and clean rooms, complemented by color-matched ridge flashings, barge boards, and gutters.

Density: 40 ± 2 kg/m³
Thermal: Extreme insulation barrier
Envelope: Complete matching flashings
THE SHREE BALAJI ADVANTAGE

Why Industry Leaders Choose Shree Balaji Infra

Trusted by pre-engineered building contractors, industrial structural consultants, and warehouse developers across Maharashtra for uncompromised steel standards and rapid jobsite dispatch.

100% Prime Mill Sourced Steel

We source exclusively from primary Tier-1 mills (Tata Steel, JSW Steel, Jindal, SAIL) backed by genuine Mill Test Certificates (MTC). Zero unverified secondary coils or substandard zinc/paint dilution guarantees decades of corrosion resistance.

Custom Lengths (Up to 14m) — Zero Scrap

Exact millimetric cut-to-length roll profiling directly at our Pune factory eliminates on-site cutting, saving structural builders up to 12% in raw material wastage and accelerating erection schedules by over 35%.

Automated Computerized Roll Profiling

Continuous computerized roll profiling lines ensure uniform pitch, controlled 550 MPa tensile yield strength, capillary anti-siphon grooves, and zero dimensional warping across every single production run.

Rapid Jobsite Logistics & Engineering Support

Located in Uruli Devachi, Pune with dedicated heavy industrial transport logistics, providing rapid dispatch across Maharashtra. Backed by free structural span estimation and wind-load consultation from our engineering team.
